1. Nigeria: Home to West Africa's largest tech ecosystem, Nigeria leads the continent in fintech innovation and startup venture funding.

2. Kenya: Renowned as the "Silicon Savannah," Kenya pioneered mobile money with M-Pesa and continues to drive advancements in digital finance and connectivity.

3. South Africa: Boasting sophisticated infrastructure, South Africa serves as a major hub for enterprise software, deep tech startups, and venture capital investment.

4. Egypt: As a rapidly expanding tech epicenter in North Africa, Egypt is experiencing unprecedented growth in e-commerce, logistics, and digital services investments.

5. Rwanda: Positioned as a regional innovation laboratory, Rwanda is actively investing in smart city technology, drone logistics, and digital governance infrastructure.
