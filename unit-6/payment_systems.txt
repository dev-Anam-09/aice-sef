Mobile money is a financial technology service that allows users to store, send, and receive money, as well as pay bills, directly through a mobile phone without needing a traditional bank account. Transactions are typically secured via text messages or mobile apps and managed by telecommunication companies or financial institutions.

In Africa, mobile money has revolutionized financial inclusion by giving tens of millions of unbanked individuals access to digital payments, peer-to-peer transfers, and micro-loans through widespread networks like M-Pesa. Today, Sub-Saharan Africa is the global leader in mobile money adoption, accounting for the majority of the world's total transaction volume and active accounts.
